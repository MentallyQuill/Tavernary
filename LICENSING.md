# Tavernary Licensing

Tavernary uses separate licenses for its software, catalog database, and
individual Kit text. These grants cover only rights that Tavernary
contributors are able to license.

- Application and software: AGPL-3.0-only
- Catalog database: ODbL-1.0
- Kit titles and descriptions: DbCL-1.0
- Tavernary name and brand assets: excluded from these grants

## Application and software

Application source, components, styles, build and validation scripts,
configuration, tests, and workflows are licensed under the GNU Affero General
Public License version 3 only. The complete terms are in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

## Catalog database

The structured catalog under `data/`, including its registry records, schemas,
vocabularies, snapshots, moderation data, and generated arrangement, is made
available under the Open Data Commons Open Database License 1.0. The complete
terms are in [`LICENSES/ODbL-1.0.txt`](LICENSES/ODbL-1.0.txt).

The ODbL applies to the database structure and arrangement to the extent those
rights exist. Tavernary does not claim ownership of third-party project names,
trademarks, repositories, project files, or unprotectable facts.

## Kit contents

Community-submitted Kit titles and descriptions are individual database
contents made available under the Open Data Commons Database Contents License
1.0. The complete terms are in
[`LICENSES/DbCL-1.0.txt`](LICENSES/DbCL-1.0.txt). GitHub attribution remains
attached to every published Kit.

## Brand and third-party materials

The Tavernary name, logos, original artwork, mascots, and distinctive visual
identity are not licensed under AGPL-3.0-only, ODbL-1.0, or DbCL-1.0 unless a
specific asset states otherwise. See [`TRADEMARKS.md`](TRADEMARKS.md).

Third-party names, marks, repository content, and project files remain the
property of their respective owners.
